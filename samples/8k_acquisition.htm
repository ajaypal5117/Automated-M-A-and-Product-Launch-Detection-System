UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

FORM 8-K

Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On October 14, 2025, Meridian Systems, Inc. (the "Company") entered into a definitive agreement providing for the acquisition of Calderon Technologies Inc., a provider of industrial sensing platforms, for total consideration of approximately $2.4 billion in cash and stock.

The purchase agreement provides that the Company will pay $1,850,000,000 in cash and issue shares valued at approximately $550 million. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approval.
