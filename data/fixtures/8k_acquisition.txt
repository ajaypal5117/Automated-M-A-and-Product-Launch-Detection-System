<p>Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.</p>
<p>On October&nbsp;14, 2025, Meridian Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the &quot;Company&quot;),
entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger providing for the acquisition of Calderon Technologies Inc.,
a provider of industrial sensing platforms, for aggregate consideration of approximately $2.4&nbsp;billion.</p>
<p>Under the terms of the Merger Agreement, the Company will pay $1,850,000,000 in cash and issue shares
of common stock valued at approximately $550 million. The common stock closed at $84.20 per share on the
trading day preceding the announcement. Calderon reported revenue of $310 million for its most recent
fiscal year.</p>
<p>The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing
conditions and receipt of required regulatory approvals.</p>
