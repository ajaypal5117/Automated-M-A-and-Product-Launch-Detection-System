<p>Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.</p>
<p>On September 3, 2025, Northgate Devices Corp. issued a press release announcing the launch of the
Atlas 700 series, a new product line of edge computing appliances. The Atlas 700 will be commercially
available in North America beginning in November 2025, with general availability in Europe and
Asia-Pacific in the first quarter of 2026.</p>
<p>Pricing for the Atlas 700 series starts at $4,200 per unit. The Company previously acquired certain
assets relating to its thermal management business in 2019.</p>
